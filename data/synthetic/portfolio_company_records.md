# Investor Portfolio & Board Disclosures — Northbeam Robotics & Lattice Metrics

Disclosed investor and board relationships for two companies referenced in Meridian SaaS Co.'s sponsor network but not owned by Meridian itself: Northbeam Robotics (an active company sharing investors with Meridian) and Lattice Metrics (Priya Anand's prior venture, acquired 2021). Unlike Meridian's own cap table (`cap_table.md`), which is private and known only through the lending relationship, these facts are the kind of information a diligence review would reasonably draw from each investor's own public portfolio disclosures and press coverage of the relevant funding rounds. All fictional, invented for this portfolio prototype.

## Section 1. Lattice Metrics — Highline Ventures
Highline Ventures' public portfolio page lists Lattice Metrics as a Series A investment, consistent with Lattice Metrics' own disclosure at the time of its 2021 acquisition. No board seat is disclosed for this investment.

## Section 2. Northbeam Robotics — Highline Ventures
Highline Ventures' public portfolio page lists Northbeam Robotics as a Seed-stage investment. Northbeam Robotics' most recent funding announcement discloses a Highline Ventures partner holding a board seat.

## Section 3. Northbeam Robotics — Anchor Point Capital
Anchor Point Capital's public portfolio page lists Northbeam Robotics as a Series A investment. Northbeam Robotics' most recent funding announcement discloses an Anchor Point Capital partner holding a board seat.
