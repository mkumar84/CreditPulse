# Meridian SaaS Co. — Founder & Executive Bios

All individuals described in this document are fictional and were invented
for this portfolio prototype. No name, biography, or history below refers
to a real person.

## Founder: Priya Anand
**Role:** Co-Founder & Chief Executive Officer
**Education:** B.S. Computer Science, University of Michigan; M.S. Computer Science, Stanford University
**Bio:** Anand co-founded Meridian SaaS Co. in 2023 after eight years building data infrastructure products. She has led Meridian's product and go-to-market strategy since inception and is Meridian's sole founder-director on the board.

### Prior Ventures
- **Lattice Metrics** (2015-2021): Co-founded and served as CEO. Lattice Metrics, a data infrastructure startup, was acquired by a public enterprise software company in 2021 for $180.0 million in total consideration. Anand held a disclosed 11.0% founder equity stake at the time of acquisition, per the acquisition's public disclosure filing.

## Founder: Marcus Webb
**Role:** Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer
**Education:** B.S. Electrical Engineering, Georgia Institute of Technology
**Bio:** Webb co-founded Meridian SaaS Co. in 2023 and leads engineering and infrastructure. He spent the prior decade building industrial software before joining Meridian.

### Prior Ventures
- **Ferrotech Systems** (2013-2019): Founded and served as CEO. Ferrotech Systems, an industrial IoT company, shut down in 2019 after failing to close a follow-on financing round; the shutdown returned no proceeds to founders or investors. In 2019, Ferrotech was the subject of an FTC inquiry into its data-handling practices, resolved via a 2020 consent agreement with no admission of wrongdoing and a modest civil settlement — see `adverse_media_records.md` for the full record.

**Role:** Chief Revenue Officer
**Education:** B.A. Economics, University of Washington
**Bio:** Ilkay joined Meridian SaaS Co. in January 2026 as its first dedicated revenue leader, most recently having spent four years as VP of Sales at Concord Systems, following a decade in enterprise sales leadership roles at larger technology companies. This is Ilkay's first founder-level equity position.
